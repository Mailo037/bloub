A little AI that lives on your desktop.

Bloub is a morphing, blinking desktop pet you can chat with. Bring any model — OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, LM Studio — and let it read files, search and help out on your machine.

On your desktop

Drop it anywhere. Talk to it right there.

Bloub floats in a transparent, frameless window. Drag it across monitors, click it to open a chat dock underneath, or right-click for the menu.

Agent tools

Helpful hands, on a short leash.

Bloub can work on your machine, within limits you set. It only sees folders you grant, edits nothing until you allow it, and has no terminal unless you switch it on. One switch turns all tools off.
